02 · 診斷 Diagnosis

先看懂：你當年其實垮了三次

Your Burnout Happened on Three Different Layers

大部分人讀你的故事只看到「太累 → 身體壞掉 → 學針灸 → 好了」。但仔細看，你的耗損是三個不同層次同時發生的，而你用了三套完全不同的方法解決。這三層就是課程的骨架。 Most people read your story as “overworked → body broke → studied acupuncture → recovered.” Look closer: three separate layers failed at once, and you fixed each one differently. Those three layers are the spine of the course.

你的針灸執照是安大略省的。但你的美國學生分散在 45 個以上不同的州，每一州對按摩師能做什麼的規定都不一樣。

所以課程裡不要說「這些你可以合法用」，那等於在跨州提供法規建議，責任會回到你身上。

Never say “you may legally use this”; that is cross-border regulatory advice and the liability lands on you. Teach pressure, angle and palpation instead, and open each module with “follow the scope of practice in your own state or province.” Identical teaching, different frame, near-zero risk.
