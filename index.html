01 · 診斷 Diagnosis

先看懂：你當年其實垮了三次

Your Burnout Happened on Three Different Layers

大部分人讀你的故事只看到「太累 → 身體壞掉 → 學針灸 → 好了」。但仔細看，你的耗損是三個不同層次同時發生的，而你用了三套完全不同的方法解決。這三層就是課程的骨架。 Most people read your story as “overworked → body broke → studied acupuncture → recovered.” Look closer: three separate layers failed at once, and you fixed each one differently. Those three layers are the spine of the course.
